# SMEAC Meeting Planner

A military-inspired meeting planning PWA built on the **SMEAC framework** (Situation, Mission, Execution, Administration, Command & Control).

Plan meetings with clarity and purpose. Based on the book [*Masterclass for Meetings*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GSM4CGDJ) by Christopher J. Riner.

## 📱 Install on Your Phone (PWA)

Once deployed, open the link on your phone:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Tap the Share icon → "Add to Home Screen"
- **Android (Chrome):** Tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home Screen"

The app works fully **offline** after install.

---

## Features

- 5-step SMEAC wizard
- Offline-capable PWA (works without internet after install)
- PDF export of your meeting plan
- "See Example" button on every step — real scenarios from the book
- Plans saved locally on your device (no account needed)
- 1 free plan per 2-week period → $10 one-time unlock for unlimited

---

## Tech Stack

- React + TypeScript + Vite
- Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui
- jsPDF for PDF export
- Service Worker for offline support
